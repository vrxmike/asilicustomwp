Client Care & Inquiries

Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, Asili Gems ships globally. All international shipments are fully insured and sent via secure courier (DHL or FedEx). Shipping times vary by location but typically range from 3-7 business days for major cities.
Are your gemstones certified?
Absolutely. Every gemstone over 1.0 carat comes with a certificate of authenticity. For our premium Tanzanite and Tsavorite collections, we provide full GIA or GRS certification detailing origin, treatment status, and grade.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 14-day return policy for all unworn items in their original condition. Because we deal in high-value custom pieces, please contact our concierge team immediately if you wish to initiate a return. Custom-commissioned pieces are final sale.
Can I customize a design?
Bespoke design is at the heart of Asili. If you see a stone you love but prefer a different setting, or if you wish to source a specific gem, our "Concierge" team works directly with you to sketch and craft your unique piece.
